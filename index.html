Historical downturn library

Context and lessons from past market shocks

Reference guide
1929–32
Great Depression-era crash

Severe equity-market collapse amid banking failures, falling demand, and deflation. Different market structure and policy environment from today.

2000–02
Dot-com bust

Valuation reset after technology-stock speculation; the broad market and individual sectors followed different paths.

2007–09
Global financial crisis

Housing-credit stress spread through leveraged financial institutions and global funding markets.

2020
COVID-19 shock

A rapid pandemic-driven selloff followed by a policy-supported recovery; timing and outcomes were highly unusual.

2022
Inflation and rate repricing

Higher interest rates pressured many equity and bond valuations, with substantial variation across assets.
